# Optimised monitor contract (opt-in, `SVA2YOSYS_SBY`)

These tests cover the optimised monitor forms (`sva2yosys.optimize.forms`, generated by `sva2yosys.codegen.systemverilog.monitor_module(machine, name, form)`) and their equivalence checks (`sva2yosys.codegen.equivalence.equivalence_check`).

Each check is an SBY job, `mode prove` with `abc pdr`. The direct and the optimised monitor are driven by the same free inputs. The job asserts, at every step, that their `fail` and `success` outputs are equal. For the `window` form, which is exact only up to the first failure, it asserts that same equality of `fail` and `success` up to and including the first failure of the direct monitor, and nothing afterwards.

## Contract

1. **Every form equals the direct monitor.** The equivalence job passes for each applicable form of these properties (all under `disable iff (rst)`, with free 1-bit inputs):
   - window and history: `a |-> ##[1:8] b`, `a |=> ##[2:5] b`, `a ##1 b |-> ##[1:3] c`, `##[1:4] a` (sequence property), `a |-> ##[1:3] $past(b)` (a condition gated at the first sample), `$rose(a) |-> ##[2:6] b`;
   - history: `a |-> ##[1:4] b ##[1:4] c`, `a |-> b[*1:3] ##1 c`, `a |-> b ##0 c ##[1:2] d`, `a |-> ##[1:3] b`, and the cover `cover property (a ##[1:3] b ##[1:2] c)`.
2. **The check is not vacuous.** Each job fails for a deliberately wrong optimised monitor: a window form whose failure age is one too early, a window form that forgets to clear the counter on a match, a window form that never reports a success (its failures are right), and a history form whose condition history is one sample short.
3. **Random properties** (marked `slow`). For 25 generated properties with at least one obligation slot (`support.ir_strategies.directives`), the job passes for every applicable form.
4. **Equivalence jobs are self-contained.** `equivalence_check` returns `monitors.sv`, `miter.sv` and `equivalence.sby`, which run as they are from one directory. It refuses the direct form, and a form that does not apply.
5. **An optimised assumption constrains like the direct one.** `assume property (a |-> ##[1:3] b)`, with `a` only at sample 0 and `b` a free input, forces `b` somewhere in samples 1–3 (a testbench assertion at sample 4 passes in BMC), and a later cover remains reachable, in the `direct` and in the `window` form.
6. **Model size** (marked `slow`; needs `SVA2YOSYS_YOSYS`). The size of a monitor is its AIG gate count: `$_AND_` and `$_NOT_` cells of the monitor alone after Yosys `prep; flatten; opt; techmap; opt -fast; aigmap; opt_clean` (`stat -json`). Run with `-s` to print the counts.
   - For each window property `a |-> ##[m:n] b` of the family in the test (`##[1:n]` for `n` from 1 to 32, and `##[2:4]`, `##[3:4]`, `##[4:8]`, `##[8:8]`, `##[2:6]`, `##[6:12]`, `##[4:32]`, all under `disable iff (rst)`), `--strategy auto` picks `window` only when the window monitor has fewer gates than the direct one, and `direct` otherwise.
   - The history monitor has more gates than the direct one for `a |-> b ##[1:4] c`, `a |-> ##[1:4] b ##[1:4] c`, `a ##1 b |-> ##[2:6] c ##1 d[*1:3]` and `a |-> ##[1:16] b`, which is why `auto` never picks it.

**Scheduling.** Named tool matrices retain small default controls and put their remaining parameters in `extended`. Every original parameter, assertion and expected result remains in the full `--slow` run; `--extended` includes the whole small matrix. Larger existing stress matrices remain `slow`.
